about

I'm eliana. I'm a student, and in the margins of that I build small static things for the web — RSS readers, image galleries, bookmark tools, book lists. Mostly vanilla JS, mostly deployed from a browser tab, mostly for an audience of one.

The through-line is collecting. I archive things: playlists that get delisted, bookmarks that rot, pages that quietly disappear. Most of what I build is downstream of wanting a better place to put all of it.

glosse — a gloss, the note in the margin explaining the word. That felt about right for a site that's mostly annotations on other people's internet.

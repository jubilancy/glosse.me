This artwork is an etching titled _Saint Christopher carrying the Christ child across a stream, another man holding a lantern at left on the riverbank_. [1]

- Artist: Rombout Eynhoudts (Dutch, 1613–1680)
- After the original work by: Peter Paul Rubens (Flemish, 1577–1640)
- Date: c. 1636–1680
- Medium: Etching on paper (held in collections such as [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/770399) and the [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1891-0414-785))
- Subject: Saint Christopher carrying the Infant Christ across a raging stream, watched by a hermit holding a lantern on the shore. [1, 2, 3]

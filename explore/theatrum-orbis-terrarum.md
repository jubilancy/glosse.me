# Theatrum Orbis Terrarum by Abraham Ortelius

![Theatrum Orbis Terrarum](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6f/OrteliusWorldMap.jpeg)


- 1570 cartographic work (atlas) by Abraham Ortelius
- Theatrum Orbis Terrarum is considered to be the first true modern atlas. Written by Abraham Ortelius, strongly encouraged by Gillis Hooftman and originally printed on 20 May 1570 in Antwerp, it consisted ...
- This is a reproduction of a 1595 map of Europe created by cartographer Abraham Ortelius.
- The original map was first published in 1570 in the _Theatrum Orbis Terrarum_, considered the first modern atlas.
- This decorative antique style print is commonly sold for home decor. [1](https://www.etsy.com/listing/888976657/1595-europe-map-print-rare-historical), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Europe), [3](https://www.maphouse.co.uk/antique-maps/europe-continent/M5336-ortelius-first-state-europe-map-1573-latin-t/), [4](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1443579852/antique-map-of-europe-reproduction-of), [5](https://www.amazon.com/Antique-Europe-Color-Poster-Print/dp/B07BSNWLZ5), [6](https://sciencephotogallery.com/featured/orteliuss-map-of-europe-1603-middle-temple-library.html)
- This image features decorative sea monsters and sirens from the _Theatrum Orbis Terrarum_, the first modern atlas created by Abraham Ortelius in 1570 [5.3, 5.6].
- Cartographers included these mythical beasts to fill uncharted or unfamiliar areas of the ocean and to appeal to the imagination of explorers [5.1].
- The illustrated creatures served to indicate potential dangers in the deep sea rather than specific geographical knowledge [5.6].

## Theater of the World

- This image is the title page for Abraham Ortelius's Theatrum Orbis Terrarum (Theater of the World), published in 1570.
- It is considered the first modern atlas, containing a collection of maps formatted into a single volume.
- The title page features allegorical figures representing the four known continents at the time: Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

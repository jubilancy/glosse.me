_**Topographia Hibernica**_ ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "Latin") for _Topography of Ireland_), also known as _**Topographia Hiberniae**_, is an account of the landscape and people of Ireland written by [Gerald of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald_of_Wales "Gerald of Wales") around 1188, soon after the [Norman invasion of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_invasion_of_Ireland "Norman invasion of Ireland"). It was the longest and most influential work on Ireland circulating in the [Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages "Middle Ages"), and its direct influence endured into the [early modern period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_modern_period "Early modern period").
